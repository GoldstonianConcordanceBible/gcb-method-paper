# 4. Interpretive Safety Constraints

Interpretive output must be constrained to prevent:
- fabricated citations
- overreach beyond evidence
- coercive claims presented as facts
- unsafe or extremist reinterpretations

Constraints include:
1) Provenance-first: every claim references anchors and evidence
2) Scope control: interpretation is labeled as hypothesis/reading, not “the only meaning”
3) Bounded certainty: confidence must reflect evidence, not rhetoric
4) Neutrality option: system can output indexing-only mode (Mirror + Water) without Fire

MWF Safety Rule: **Fire is optional; Mirror and Water are mandatory.**
