# 7. Theological Non-Assertion Clause

MWF is an interpretive operating system for indexing and structured study.

It does not assert:
- exclusive theological truth claims as facts
- authority to adjudicate denominations or traditions
- replacement of clergy, communities, or scholarship

MWF can host interpretations, but interpretations must be labeled, evidenced, and reviewable. The system defaults to neutral indexing outputs suitable for education and AI retrieval.
