# 8. Use in Educational and AI Systems

## 8.1 Education
MWF supports:
- curriculum alignment (anchor-based lesson plans)
- reading comprehension (Mirror outputs)
- comparative tradition studies (Water outputs)
- reflective writing (Fire outputs with constraints)

## 8.2 AI systems
MWF supports:
- RAG retrieval with deterministic anchor objects
- provenance logging and audit trails
- safe “index-only” modes for sensitive contexts
- schema-bound prompt recipes for consistent behavior

## 8.3 Governance
Recommended:
- contribution-only governance for link proposals
- attestation logs for changes
- release tags with DOI snapshots
